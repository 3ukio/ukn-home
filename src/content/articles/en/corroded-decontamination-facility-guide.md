**Keep a rock for Klawfly, follow Blatt’s colour call, and clear Bargott’s bombs.** Boss notes use the supplied third-party walkthrough. Footage version and Global behaviour have not been independently play-tested.

## STEP 01 — Devouring Klawfly

<button type="button" data-video-jump="0">▷ 00:00 — Devouring Klawfly</button>

### 00:18 — Numbered jumps and rocks

Four numbered players bait jumps through unwanted rocks. **Leave at least one rock intact**, then hide behind it when the boss returns centrally for the large AoE. Excess intact rocks may leave permanent pollution; do not preserve every rock either.

<button type="button" data-video-jump="18">▷ 00:18</button>

### 00:45 — Purple orbs, around 50% HP

Eight numbered players handle eight central orbs **in number order, during the purple state**. Avoid the temporary red state: a red pickup causes raid-wide damage. Orbs left to reach the edge may wipe the group. Agree on assignments and move promptly.

<button type="button" data-video-jump="45">▷ 00:45</button>

Keep a rock for the AoE, then take purple orbs in order.

## STEP 02 — Black Blood Blatt

<button type="button" data-video-jump="100">▷ 01:40 — Black Blood Blatt</button>

### 01:40 — Red / violet order, around 75% HP

Listen to the call: Violet means **violet → red**; Red means **red → violet**. The matching glow is a secondary cue. **Bait line attacks away from the colour zones before entering them**; damaging a zone can disrupt the check.

### 02:56 — Clear pollution

Remove pollution before it grows into tentacles. After handling one patch, **wait for your overhead marker to disappear** before clearing the next.

### 03:22 — SoS slime barriers, around 30% HP

Four slimes create brief barriers when killed—**about 3 seconds in the walkthrough**, not an official measured duration. Keep damaging line AoEs away from them. Later white lines heal slimes; the demonstrated response is to stand in the path to intercept them, pending live confirmation. Keep slimes low and **kill them when Fierce Toxic Aura Wave starts casting**, then shelter in the barrier. Do not kill all four on spawn.

<button type="button" data-video-jump="202">▷ 03:22</button>

Listen to colours, clear pollution, and time the slime kill.

<a id="step-03"></a>

## STEP 03 — Terminator Bargott

<button type="button" data-video-jump="275">▷ 04:35 — Terminator Bargott</button>

### 04:35 — Permanent pollution

Targeted attacks leave permanent pollution. Baiting these toward the edge preserves central space; this is a positioning suggestion, not the only solution.

### 04:52 — Three shared hits

A grabbed player triggers **three ground slams** with a large red shared-damage marker. **Enter the share area** to distribute damage. This red circle is not one to run away from.

### 05:10 — Eight numbered rolls

Eight numbered players guide sequential rolls **through the bombs**. Coordinate your lines; leftover bombs later explode for raid-wide damage.

### 05:41 — Blue-orb blind

A blue weapon and cue precede a slam that creates blue orbs. **Turn your character away from the orb** to avoid the facing check.

<button type="button" data-video-jump="341">▷ 05:41</button>

### Around 06:09 — Rotating chains

Marked chain carriers use the rotating chains to destroy bombs. Stay apart: collisions between carriers can stun them and remove the chain effect.

### 06:43 — Final bomb throws

When the boss jumps outside and pulls players aside: **approach a bomb → interact to pick it up → approach and target the boss → interact to throw**. The video uses F; use your own binding. Its “8” is a special throw-reduced health counter, not ordinary HP of 8. Earlier mechanics may repeat afterwards.

<button type="button" data-video-jump="403">▷ 06:43</button>

Clear pollution and bombs, face away from blue orbs, then throw bombs.

## Event rules and rewards

Menu → Sanctuary → Corroded Decontamination Facility. Stats are normalized to the boss. The event starts after **Nov 25 maintenance**, ending **Dec 15 before maintenance (PST) / Dec 16 before maintenance (CET)**. Japanese lists Dec 16 without a maintenance time zone.

**Official version note:** English leaves the cube's Odyle cost as “x”; Japanese says no cost. Check the live in-game prompt rather than assuming a universal rule.
