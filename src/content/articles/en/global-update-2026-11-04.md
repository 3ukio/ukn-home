## Key changes

- **Abyssal Forge: Ludra** opens with Normal and Hard modes, a **1-hour limit**, and entry item levels of **2,800 / 3,000**.
- New bracelets support Amplify levels **3 / 5**. Chaotic Outer Reshanta offers standardized PvP stats.
- Outer Reshanta PvP Abyss Points fall **50%**; Abyss Corridor points per run rise **50%** as Artifact events move to **Thursday and Saturday**.
- Exploration rewards improve, and four Ludra-related event/reward sections are detailed below.

## Sanctuary: Abyssal Forge: Ludra

Normal and Hard share the same **types** of item rewards, but drop rates differ. Hard is designed for advanced character progression, mechanic knowledge and Force coordination. The entry threshold alone does not guarantee a clear.

| Difficulty | Minimum item level | Run limit |
|---|---:|---|
| Normal | **2,800** | **1 hour** |
| Hard | **3,000** | **1 hour** |

Rewards include **Ludra's Weapon and Guard (Bound)**, **Ludra's Bracelet (Bound)**, **Abyssal Bracelet (Bound)**, **Sealed Abyssal Waves Wings**, and **Artwork: Abyssal Ludra**. Substance Morph using a Gear Change Voucher can produce an **Abyssal Forge: Weapon Selection Chest (Bound)**.

Top Hard-clearing Forces enter the regional Hall of Fame. Duplicate character records may be removed; records may also be removed for prohibited activities.

[Abyssal Forge: Ludra Guide](/en/articles/abyssal-forge-ludra-guide/)

## Bracelet and voucher exchanges

- **Abyssal Bracelet (Bound)**: Amplify up to **Level 3**; can drop in both difficulties.
- **Ludra's Bracelet (Bound)**: Amplify up to **Level 5**.
- Normal guaranteed rewards offer **Gear Change Voucher: Ludra (Bound)**; Hard offers **Gear Change Voucher: Ludra (Hard) (Bound)**.
- Substance Morph: **6** Normal vouchers → **1 Ludra's Bracelet (Bound)**, or **3** Hard vouchers → **1 Ludra's Bracelet (Bound)**.
- **2** Normal vouchers → **1** Hard voucher through Substance Morph.

## Abyss and PvP

### Chaotic Outer Reshanta

Entry begins at **Lv. 45**, with **3 hours per week** of base playtime; Outer Entry Stones extend it. Character stats, skill levels and Stigma levels are standardized. Only stat bonuses and specialization effects from class-specific skills and Stigmas apply. Some Season Missions can be completed here.

Outer Reshanta rewards include Kina (Bound), Abyss Points, Common/Rare/Epic Gear, Outer Abyss Rift Stone (Bound), and other bound crafting materials. **Abyss Points earned through PvP in Outer Reshanta decrease by 50%.**

### Artifact events

- Artifact Siege: **3 times weekly → 2**, on **Thursdays and Saturdays at 21:00**.
- **Abyss Corridor Abyss Points per run increase by 50%.**
- Executor Kaira, Executor Argo and Executor Tamasa follow the same **Thursday/Saturday** schedule, at **21:30**.

The notice does not specify a time zone for these 21:00/21:30 times.

## Field Boss schedules

- Elyos: Eternal Gartua / High Commander Lagta respawn interval **12 → 11 hours**.
- Asmodian: Immortal Gartua / High Commander Lagta respawn interval **12 → 11 hours**.
- Abyss Field Boss **Argo, the Spirit King** spawns **twice daily, at 10:30 and 22:30**. The notice does not specify a time zone.

## Expedition (Exploration) rewards

Applies to **Draupnir**, **Vakron Sky Island** and **Ferocious Horn Den**, all in **Exploration** mode:

- Available rewards increase to match **Normal** difficulty. Kina (Bound), Enhance Stones, Silentium (Bound) and Soul Codex (Bound) quantities increase.
- **Amplify Stone Fragment (Unique) (Bound)** is added.
- Refining Stones and crafting materials drop in the same quantities as Normal, but as **bound, non-tradable items**.
- Guaranteed-reward run requirements and available run counts are adjusted to match **Conquest** difficulty.

| Cumulative Exploration runs | Kina received |
|---|---:|
| 0–40 | **100%** |
| 41–50 | **80%** |
| 51–60 | **60%** |
| 61–70 | **40%** |
| 71+ | **20%** |

## UI improvements

The Color Palette gains a **Standard Color Code** input for color codes used in web searches and other applications. Earlier color codes remain usable through the Standard Color Code field. Applies to **Customization**, **Closet**, and **Settings → Chat Font Color**.

## Events

### Ludra Conquest Check-In

**Nov 4, 2026, 06:00 PST – Nov 17, 23:59 PST** / **Nov 4, 15:00 CET – Nov 18, 08:59 CET**.

Check in **once daily**. Each check-in unlocks the next day's reward. There are **7 rewards across 14 days**, so up to **7 missed days** still allows completion.

| Day | Reward | Quantity |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Bound Kina | **500,000** |
| 2 | Centuryroot Token (Shugo Coin) | **2,000** |
| 3 | Phantasmal Fragment (Nightmare Coin) | **3,000** |
| 4 | Abyss Points | **30,000** |
| 5 | Soul Crystal | **3,000** |
| 6 | Bound Kina | **1,000,000** |
| 7 | Fresh Leaf Tiara (Helm) | **1** |

### Odyle Energy Boost

Official schedule: **Oct 28 after maintenance – Nov 10 before maintenance (labelled PST)** / **Oct 28 after maintenance – Nov 11 before maintenance (CET)**. No clock times are supplied.

Log in each day and claim **Odyle Energy ×4** from in-game mail. **One delivery per account per day**; each mail expires **7 days after receipt**.

### Ludra Hunt

**Nov 4 after maintenance – Nov 24 before maintenance (PST)** / **Nov 4 after maintenance – Nov 25 before maintenance (CET)**. Regions: **Europe, NA East, NA West, South America and Asia**.

The first **20 Hard-clearing parties per region** receive ranked titles. Other players who clear Normal or Hard during the event also receive a title.

| Regional Hard clear placement | Eligible players per region | Title |
|---|---:|---|
| 1st party | **10** | The First to Slay Ludra |
| 2nd–5th parties | **40** | Ludra's Conqueror |
| 6th–20th parties | **150** | Ludra's Vanquisher |
| Other Normal/Hard clears | All other qualifying players | Ludra's Nemesis |

Ranks **1–3** have a **purple decorative background**. Clears must occur during the event. Titles are sent **no later than Dec 2, 23:59 PST**.

### Abyssal Refinement Clear During AA Period

The **10 EU Force members** who cleared Ludra during AA receive the red-background title **The First to Seal Ludra**. This is separate from the current Ludra Hunt rankings.

## Other improvements and restoration

- Some Pet souls previously restricted to Pet Chests can drop directly from **Expedition and Transcendence Dungeons**.
- New **100%-success** recipes for **True Dragon and Star Dragon Gear**: no **COMBO (Great Success)**, and results are **Bound and non-tradable**. Both Bound and Unbound Artisan-to-Epic Gear can be used as materials.
- Existing recipes remain: they can fail, but retain COMBO bonus rewards and tradable Gear outcomes. The new guaranteed recipes forgo those benefits.
- **Item Source** adds dungeon difficulty information.
- During the Nov 4 update, **EU** players receive content entry passes that failed to reset as intended on **Oct 25**.
