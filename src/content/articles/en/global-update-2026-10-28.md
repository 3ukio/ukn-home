## Highlights

- **Hard mode opens for Urugugu Canyon and Vakron Sky Island at item level 2700.**
- Boss reductions depend on difficulty and Transcendence stage; they are not one universal adjustment.
- All-class changes affect **base HP at Level 45**. The individual skill values listed this week concern Assassin.

## Detailed changes

### Expedition and Transcendence bosses

- **Normal Expedition and Transcendence Stage 1:** Boss Attack decreases by **15%**.
- **Hard Expedition and Transcendence Stages 2–4:** Boss Attack and Damage Boost each decrease by **10%**.
- **Transcendence Stages 1–2:** Boss HP decreases by **6.5%**.

These rules apply to their named modes and stages, not to every encounter alike.

### Two new Hard Expeditions

**Urugugu Canyon** and **Vakron Sky Island** gain Hard mode.

- Entry requires **item level 2700**.
- Bosses enter Frenzy after **4 minutes**.
- Each player has a maximum of **3 revives**.
- The corresponding dungeon guarantees Ariel Fragment: Urugugu or Ariel Fragment: Vakron.
- The per-character conversion ceiling remains **30 Daevanion Crystals: Ariel**.

### Daily Dungeon

**Odylium Repository** is added. Clear points determine the Soul Crystal (Bound) rewards associated with pets. Its entry count is shared with **Daeva Bio-Research Base**.

### Crafted gear progression

The adjustment concerns the **Amplify cap**, rather than a blanket change to other enhancement systems.

- True Dragon and Star Dragon, including their Splendent variants: **maximum Amplify level 1 → 3**.
- White Dragon and Dark Dragon, including their Splendent variants: **maximum Amplify level 3 → 5**.
- Transfer becomes available from **Tier 3 Expedition gear to Tier 2 Crafted gear**.
- Transfer also becomes available from **Tier 2 Expedition gear to Tier 1 Crafted gear**.

## Class and skill changes

### Base HP across all classes

At **Level 45**:

- **Gladiator and Templar:** gain **1,500 base HP**, reaching **6,202**.
- The other six classes gain **1,000 base HP**, reaching **5,702**.

**This is a base-HP adjustment, not an increase to every class's skill damage.**

### Assassin

- **Heightened Sixth Sense:** the HP effect becomes **1,000 / 2,000 / 4,000** at skill **Levels 5 / 10 / 20**, respectively.
- **Illusive Clone:** base cooldown falls from **90 seconds to 60 seconds**.

These two Assassin entries are the explicitly listed individual skill-value changes. No additional class buffs are inferred here.

## Systems and bug fixes

### Gathering and character rules

- The minimum Gathering level rises from **10 to 45**.
- Gathering is limited to **1,000 times per server per day**.
- The stated daily reset is **00:00 PDT / 08:00 CET**. These retain the official time zones; they are not JST.
- Character deletion waits increase from **1 day to 7 days**.
- Inactivity returns players to the lobby after **30 minutes**, down from **3 hours**.
- Hidden Cubes appear more frequently; no numerical increase is specified.

### Abyss

- More players can enter the Abyss.
- Lower Abyss Dimensional Core HP increases.
- Once an Artifact Battle lasts **10 minutes or longer**, **Dissipation of Power** weakens the Dimensional Core.

### Fixes

- Retrying an Expedition Dungeon on Hard no longer occasionally leaves the resurrection count unreset.
- A problem making some player characters invisible in PvP is addressed.
- The swapped Ariel and Azphel Daevanion positions in the Character Progression summary are corrected.
