## Update highlights

- **Ludra Normal and Hard: 2-hour limit**, downscaled Central Abyss Fusion Reactor HP, and **Reward Group 5: 30% → 100%**.
- **Fire Temple / Ferocious Horn Den Hard** opens at **item level 2,800**, with a **4-minute boss Frenzy timer** and **3 revives per player**.
- Selected consumables last **10 minutes** and survive death; weekly progression rewards improve.

## Ludra raid rewards

Both difficulties move from **1 to 2 hours**. Only the central reactor's HP reduction is specified; this is not a redesign of every final-boss mechanic.

Reward Group 5 now includes **Wrathful Mind (Bound), Wrathful Will (Bound), Wrathful Ego (Bound)**. **100% is the group's acquisition chance, not a guarantee of every material in it.**

## Two Hard Expeditions

Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Den share the entry and combat limits above.

- **Philosopher's Stone: Revelation (Bound)** can drop by chance and can also be selected as a guaranteed reward.
- Each dungeon's corresponding **Ariel Fragment (Bound) has a 100% drop chance**.
- Expedition Hero Artwork can drop by chance. Kina rewards exceed Normal.
- All Unique crafting materials become available, including Wrathful Mind, Will and Ego. No individual drop rates are specified.
- Hard achievements award Unique Skins.

## Abyss, consumables and weekly rewards

Only **Krao Cave Conquest and Draupnir Conquest** Kina rewards and **monster Kina in the Lower Abyss** change to Bound. This does not make all Kina untradeable.

Chaotic Outer Reshanta grants more Abyss Points; the notice gives no numerical increase.

Potions, food and scrolls covered by the **5-minute effect** adjustment now last **10 minutes**. Their effects persist through death and resurrection; this is not a change to every consumable.

- Transcendence weekly Season Missions, **Way of the Transcended Steps 1–3**: **1 Sync Stone (Unique) (Bound) each, up to 3 weekly**.
- Shugo Festival gains weekly Season Missions with additional Abyss Points.
- Duties Abyss Points: **1,000 → 2,500**.

## Fixes and restoration

Dungeon graphics assets preload to reduce temporary freezes. Fixes address the underwater gauge decreasing on entry to some underwater Seal Dungeons, blocked progression in the Asmodian quest dungeon **Zikel's Sanctum**, and **Ludra's Trial** failing to reset after a mechanic wipe.

**Only characters affected by the Nov 4 reduction in Exploration guaranteed-reward requirements** receive the restoration: corresponding dungeon **Armor Selection Chests (Bound)** delivered to Cube (Inventory) during Nov 11 maintenance. This is not a reward for all players.

New cosmetic sales are also listed; no class skill balance changes appear in this patch.
