## Highlights

- **Krao Cave and Draupnir gain Hard mode at item level 2600.**
- The Ariel Daevanion Board introduces fragment-based progression.
- Class entries fix skill behavior or presentation. **They are not numerical class-damage adjustments.**

## Detailed changes

### Hard Expeditions

**Krao Cave** and **Draupnir** receive Hard mode.

- Entry requires **item level 2600**.
- The boss Frenzy timer is **4 minutes**.
- Each player has a maximum of **3 revives**.

### Hard-mode rewards

- Kina rewards are higher than on Normal difficulty.
- Philosopher's Stone: Revelation (Bound) can drop and can also be selected as a guaranteed reward.
- Ariel Fragments (Bound) are available, with the fragment type depending on the dungeon.
- Expedition Hero Artwork can be obtained as a chance reward.
- Relevant Unique crafting materials are obtainable, including Wrathful Mind, Wrathful Will and Wrathful Ego.
- New Expedition (Hard) achievements provide corresponding Unique appearance rewards.

No drop percentages or unspecified reward quantities are assumed here.

### Ariel Daevanion Board

The new **Ariel Daevanion Board** uses fragments earned from Hard Expeditions and converted through Substance Morph.

- **100 Ariel Fragments → 1 Daevanion Crystal: Ariel (Bound)**.
- This conversion is limited to **30 times per character**. Dungeon-specific fragment acquisition or conversion limits also apply.
- **1 Ariel Fragment → 5 Enhance Stones** can be converted without a limit on the number of conversions.

## Class and skill fixes

### Sorcerer

- **Cold Storm:** extra damage now works against targets with stacked **Frostbite**.
- Frostbite's effect is corrected to match the skill level.
- With **Specialty 1**, targets in the area no longer receive another Frostbite application after Cold Storm's area effect ends.
- **Fire Wall:** extra damage is restricted to targets affected by **Embers**, correcting its application to unaffected targets.

### Chanter

- **Spinning Strike:** a problem with skill-effect display is corrected.

These are behavior and visual fixes, **not a general numerical increase to class damage**.

## Systems and bug fixes

### Abyss and Market

- More players can enter the Abyss.
- Lower Abyss Dimensional Core HP and the HP of all bosses spawning in the Abyss increase.
- The collision hitboxes of **Kaira** and **Argo** become slightly larger.
- Restrictions on listing gathered materials in the Market are adjusted according to gameplay history.
- Some previously restricted players can trade normally again; **restrictions are not lifted for everyone**.

### Other fixes

- Empyrean Traces obstructed by Halloween decorations at Dawn Legion Base and Zumion Village in Verteron are moved to accessible locations.
- A duplicated movement-control option in settings is corrected.
- A mismatch between a title's actual effect and the item-tooltip description is corrected.
