## Check the edition first

This site covers AION2 Global / Steam / PURPLE. A detail published for the Korean or Taiwanese edition is not automatically a Global feature. Treat the interface language and the applicable server region as separate things.

## Follow the official source

Start with the [Global official website](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/index?redirect=false), [official notices](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/list) and [official Steam news](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3393110). Check the publication date, affected region and any amendments before relying on an announcement.

Our summaries are independently written. We do not reproduce entire announcements or reuse another site's guides. Return to the original notice when checking an exact condition or time.

## When a detail is unconfirmed

An unknown official timestamp stays unknown. An expected maintenance end time does not establish actual completion. Restriction counts for RMT or bots belong to individual announcements; adding separate batches does not establish a unique-account total.
