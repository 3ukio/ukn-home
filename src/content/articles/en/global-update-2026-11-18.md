## Key changes

- **Ludra Easy**: **2 hours**, **item level 2,800**, **unlimited personal revives**.
- Ludra gear's **combined drop rate: Normal/Hard 5% → 15%; Easy 15%**.
- Weapon selection chest recipes cost **5,000,000 Kina instead of 10,000,000**.

## Ludra Easy

The final Ludra encounter retains Normal's HP, with easier mechanic handling and adjusted stats that make critical hits easier. This does not mean every boss has identical HP or that all mechanics are unchanged.

Easy rewards include **Ludra's Weapon (Bound)** and **Ludra's Guard (Bound)**.

**15% is the combined gear drop rate**, not 15% for each item or a guaranteed reward per player. It does not replace the separate **Reward Group 5 100%** rule introduced on Nov 11.

## Weapon selection chest exchanges

Each recipe below produces **1 Abyssal Forge: Weapon Selection Chest (Bound)** and requires **5,000,000 Kina**. These are separate recipes; do not mix voucher difficulties.

| Gear Change Voucher: Ludra (Bound) difficulty | Required vouchers |
|---|---:|
| Hard | **1** |
| Normal | **2** |
| Easy | **4** |

Players who already obtained the corresponding chest using a Hard voucher receive **1 Gear Change Voucher: Ludra [Hard] (Bound)** retroactively. Restoration applies to affected previous exchanges, not everyone. The notice gives no claim portal or separate payout date.

## Abyss Points boost

**3× AP** applies only to Outer Abyss monster drops, Battlefield Victory Participation Rewards and Battlefield Victory Mission Rewards. **The AP cap does not change; Outer Abyss PvP AP is excluded.**

The English schedule states:
- **PST: Nov 17 after maintenance → Dec 16 before maintenance, 2026**.
- **CET: Nov 18 after maintenance → Dec 17 before maintenance, 2026**.

The Japanese notice instead lists **Nov 18 after maintenance → Dec 16 before maintenance**, without an explicit time zone. These date ranges differ; no single converted global deadline is confirmed here.

## Gathering conversion and fixes

The English PDF describes higher-grade gathering materials converting into **2 lower-grade materials**, without listing grades. The Japanese PDF lists **Unique ×1 → Epic ×3**, **Epic ×1 → Rare ×2**, **Rare ×1 → Common ×2**. **The Unique → Epic quantity conflicts and needs confirmation**; do not assume a universal conversion ratio.

Fixes address Auto-Translation leaving text untranslated, chat jumping to the bottom while reading history, and some Ludra attacks not executing correctly. No specific replacement boss strategy is provided.

## Field Kina and restoration

Kina from **all field monsters** changes to **Kina (Bound)**. The corresponding **daily 1,000,000 Kina acquisition limit** also uses Bound Kina. This is not a statement that every Kina source is now bound.

Players affected by the earlier Ludra Weapon Exchange Ticket morph recipe receive the difference. Keep this limited to affected exchanges; the specific Hard voucher restoration is described above.
