## At a glance

**Four classes change; equip up to 5 Stigma Skills.** World Market and World Exchange arrive with a **20% transaction tax**. A new Daily Dungeon shares existing entries, while the specified Transcendence missions now award up to **12 Sync Stones a week**.

## Class changes

| Class / skill | Change |
|---|---|
| Templar — Judgment | PvP damage approximately **−5%**; Specialty 2 bonus PvP damage against Impact-affected targets approximately **−10%**; Specialty 3 on-hit PvP bonus approximately **−10%**. |
| Ranger — Marking Shot | Specialty 1 Perfect Chance while active: **+5% → +10%**. |
| Ranger — Bow of Blessing | Cooldown **2 minutes → 1 minute 30 seconds**. |
| Chanter — Incandescent Blow / Bursting Blow | Specialty 5 still lands as Multi-Hit, but no longer ignores Block and Evasion. |
| Chanter — Spinning Strike | Specialty 5 still lands as Critical Hit, but no longer ignores Block and Evasion. |
| Spiritmaster — Cold Shock / Vacuum Explosion / Earth Tremor | Specialty 4's Earth Tremor on-hit Attack increase per stack **2% → 10%**, maximum stacks **5 → 1**. The Attack increase is no longer removable by buff-removal skills. |
| Spiritmaster — Element Unification | Level 1 Critical Hit Damage bonus **1.1% → 5.5%**; per-level increase **0.1% → 0.5%**. The bonus no longer stacks. |

## World trade and Negative Kina

Both **World Market and World Exchange charge 20% per transaction**.

Accounts involved in unauthorized transactions may receive Negative Kina. While the balance is negative, market purchasing, listing and settlement; Exchange listing and settlement; and server transfers are unavailable. Earning **unbound Kina** through legitimate activities such as hunting, Odyle Energy Cubes or Kina chests restores the balance. Restrictions lift at **zero or above**.

## Stigma and weekly rewards

- Equip up to **5 Stigma Skills**. Additional acquisition sources total **50 Stigma Shards (Bound)**; these are acquisition opportunities, not a direct gift.
- Nightmare Dungeons Shop and Shugo Festival Shop: each character's shard purchase limit **35 → 50**.
- Wind Breeze Merchants, Special: **20 per character**, **250,000 Kina each**.
- Transcendence Dungeon Season Missions, **Way of the Transcended Steps 1–3**: **4 Sync Stones (Unique) (Bound) each**, weekly maximum **3 → 12**.

## Daily Dungeon and Abyss Corridor

**Crobakhi's Secret Depository** grants Kina (Bound) according to clear points. It shares entries with **Daeva Bio-Research Base and Odylium Repository**.

If the opposing faction captures every Artifact during Artifact Siege, an Abyss Corridor portal appears at the disadvantaged faction's base. Entering under that condition applies **−30% Abyss Point gains**.

Attendance, the event pass and Corroded Decontamination Facility start after **Nov 25 maintenance**. The English end dates are **Dec 15 before maintenance (PST) / Dec 16 before maintenance (CET)**. Japanese lists Dec 16 without an explicit maintenance time zone; keep the original regional dates.

Maximum level **20**; further EXP gives repeatable rewards, **Resurrection Spiritstone (Bound) ×1** per track. Weekly cap **15,000 XP per server**; buying a level costs **50 Quna**.

The Basic level 10 Resurrection Spiritstone quantity differs: English **1**, Japanese **2**. That cell remains unconfirmed.
